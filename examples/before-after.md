# Original demonstration text

This is original text written for the project. It is not taken from a YouTube
video. The demo PDF links to the yt-dlp test video to demonstrate annotations;
those links do not locate the original practice sentences.

## Raw automatic-caption style

```text
you don't have to understand every word on the first try
notice which words the speaker stresses and where they pause
then say the sentence aloud following the same rhythm
that's a very very important part of learning natural speech
```

## Shadowing handout

04. You don't have to understand every word on the first try.
05. Notice which words the speaker stresses, and where they pause.
06. Then say the sentence aloud, following the same rhythm.
09. That's a very, very important part of learning natural speech.

Punctuation improves readability while contractions and the repeated “very” stay.

Chinese title glyphs, selectable text and note space.
